# Evidence Preservation and Containment

## Triage steps
Establish the alert scope and timeline, identify the data owner and system owner, and check for corroborating events. Separate observed facts from hypotheses and note gaps in collection.

## Evidence preservation
Keep original event records and their identifiers, retain timestamps with timezone context, capture query and export details, and store collected material in access-controlled evidence storage. Follow retention and chain-of-custody requirements.

## Containment options
Isolation, credential changes, egress restrictions, or process termination are recommendations for analyst approval, never automatic actions by this assistant. Consider service dependencies and evidence volatility before acting.

## Escalation
Escalate suspected data loss, privileged-account misuse, active destructive behavior, or uncertainty that exceeds the team's authority to the incident lead and relevant system owner.
