# Meaning of UNKNOWN_CANDIDATE

`UNKNOWN_CANDIDATE` means the open-set model's top known-class margin is low enough to reject a confident known-family assignment under the configured rule. It is a review signal, not a confirmed novel attack and not proof that the traffic is malicious. Check the stored score, margin, threshold, event features, and surrounding telemetry before drawing a conclusion.
